# DEC-B08: Late fees (revised)

Status: accepted (2026-03-05, owner: Finance). Supersedes DEC-B03.

An invoice that is still unpaid after its due date is charged a late fee of 1.5% of the invoice amount, rounded to
the cent (half away from zero). The fee is charged once per invoice, when late fees are run, and then forms part of
what is outstanding on the invoice.
