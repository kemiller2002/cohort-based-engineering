# DEC-004: Renewals

Status: accepted (2026-01-12, owner: Head of Lending). Revision 2 (2026-03-01): renewed
due dates fall on open days. Revision 3 (2026-04-01, Head of Lending): a renewal adds the
borrower's loan period.

A loan may be renewed at most 2 times. Each renewal moves the due date one loan period
of the borrower's member type (DEC-001) later than the current due date; when the
library is closed on that day, the due date is the next day the library is open. A loan
cannot be renewed:

1. when it is overdue (today is after its due date);
2. when it has already been renewed 2 times;
3. when any member holds the copy's book.

When several of these apply, the first one in this list is the reason given.
