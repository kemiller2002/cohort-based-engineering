# CLM-15: Settlement statements

Requirements: CLM-15-R1, CLM-15-R2, CLM-15-R3. Owner: Claims Operations.

## CLM-15-R1

A settlement statement is produced for each claim: the replacement cost of each item less its depreciation, summed,
less the deductible that applies to the claim, never below zero.

## CLM-15-R2

The statement prints the depreciation taken on each item, held to the cent.

## CLM-15-R3

A claim whose loss date falls outside the policy's term is refused.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
