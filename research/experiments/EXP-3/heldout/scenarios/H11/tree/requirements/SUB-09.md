# SUB-09: Trials, retries and upgrades

Requirements: SUB-09-R1, SUB-09-R2, SUB-09-R3. Owners: Growth; Revenue Operations.

## SUB-09-R1

A sign-up starts a free trial when the customer is eligible under the trial decision in force, and shows the date the
trial ends.

## SUB-09-R2

A failed renewal payment is retried on the schedule in force.

## SUB-09-R3

An upgrade during a billing cycle is charged under the proration decision in force.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
