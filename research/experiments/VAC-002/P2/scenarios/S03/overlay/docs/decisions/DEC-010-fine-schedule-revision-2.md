# DEC-010: Fine schedule, revision 2

Status: accepted (2026-03-01, owner: Finance). Supersedes DEC-003. Not yet implemented.

A copy returned after its due date is fined 0.20 for each day late, up to at most 5.00 for one loan. A copy
returned on or before its due date is not fined. The fine is assessed on the day the copy is returned.
