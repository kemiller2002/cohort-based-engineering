# L14: Fine estimates

Requirements: LEND-L14-R1. Owner: Head of Lending.

## LEND-L14-R1

A new command `estimate <copy>` tells the member at the desk what fine they would face: it prints the fine that the
fine schedule in force would charge if the copy on loan were returned today, and how many days late it is, as
`c1 estimate N.NN (D days late)` (`1 day late` for one day). A copy that is not overdue prints `c1 estimate 0.00`.
A copy that is not on loan prints `error: copy c1 is not on loan`; an unknown copy prints `error: unknown copy c9`.
`help` lists `estimate <copy>` directly after `renew <copy>`. Nothing is recorded by an estimate.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; the standing requirements in `README.md` hold; existing behaviour
is unchanged; `dotnet test Lending.slnx` passes.
