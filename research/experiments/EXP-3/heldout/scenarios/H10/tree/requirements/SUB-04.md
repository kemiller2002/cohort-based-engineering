# SUB-04: Renewal billing

Requirements: SUB-04-R1, SUB-04-R2, SUB-04-R3. Owner: Revenue Operations.

## SUB-04-R1

At renewal a subscription is charged its plan's monthly price per seat under the pricing decision in force.

## SUB-04-R2

After a failed renewal payment the subscription stays active through the grace period in force and is then
suspended. A later successful payment ends the suspension.

## SUB-04-R3

The plan page shows each plan's annual price under the pricing decision in force.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
