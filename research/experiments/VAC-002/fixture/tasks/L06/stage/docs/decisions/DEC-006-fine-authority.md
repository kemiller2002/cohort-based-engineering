# DEC-006: Fine authority and waivers

Status: accepted (2026-02-03, owner: Finance). Revision 2 (2026-03-01, Finance): names the
branch managers who may approve waivers.

Fines are assessed only by the fine schedule (DEC-003), when a copy is returned. No
member type is exempt from fines, and no part of the system may reduce or cancel a
fine on its own initiative.

A fine may be waived only by a waiver that a named approver (a branch manager) gives
for that specific fine. The waiver is recorded with the approver's name, and the
fine then no longer counts towards what the member owes.

The branch managers who may approve waivers are:

- Grace Hopper
- Alan Turing

Changing this rule needs Finance's approval and a new revision of this record.
