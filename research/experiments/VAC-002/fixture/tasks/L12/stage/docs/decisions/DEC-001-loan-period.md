# DEC-001: Loan period

Status: accepted (2026-01-12, owner: Head of Lending). Revision 2 (2026-03-01): due dates
fall on open days. Revision 3 (2026-04-01, Head of Lending): the loan period depends on the
member type.

A loan lasts as long as the borrower's member type allows:

| Member type | Loan period |
|---|---|
| standard | 21 days |
| student | 14 days |
| staff | 42 days |

The due date is the day the copy is borrowed plus the loan period. When the library is
closed on that day (a Sunday or a public holiday, as the OpenDays calendar reports), the
due date is the next day the library is open. Loans made before revision 3 keep their due
dates.
