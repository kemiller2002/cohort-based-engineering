# PRC-12: Purchase order issue and landed cost

Requirements: PRC-12-R1, PRC-12-R2, PRC-12-R3. Owners: Procurement Office; Treasury.

## PRC-12-R1

A purchase order is issued only to a vendor the debarment decision in force allows.

## PRC-12-R2

Each requisition shows its landed-cost estimate: the total plus the freight allowance for its incoterm under the
freight decision in force.

## PRC-12-R3

A paid invoice records the early-payment discount it earned under the discount decision in force.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
