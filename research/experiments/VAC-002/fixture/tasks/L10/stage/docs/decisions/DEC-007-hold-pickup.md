# DEC-007: Hold pickup window

Status: accepted (2026-02-17, owner: Head of Lending). Revision 2 (2026-03-20, Head of
Lending): pickup deadlines fall on open days.

When a copy of a book with holds comes back, it is set aside for the member at the
head of the book's queue. That member has 7 days to collect it: the pickup deadline
is the return day plus 7 days; when the library is closed on that day, the deadline is
the next day the library is open. A hold that is not collected by the end of its pickup
deadline expires and is removed from the queue.
