# CLM-12: Referral list and acknowledgement tracking

Requirements: CLM-12-R1, CLM-12-R2, CLM-12-R3. Owners: Claims Operations; Special Investigations.

## CLM-12-R1

A daily referral list shows each open claim that must go to Special Investigations under the referral decision in
force, oldest report first, with the date its review is due.

## CLM-12-R2

Each newly reported claim gets an acknowledgement deadline under the acknowledgement decision in force. The list
flags every claim whose deadline has passed.

## CLM-12-R3

A claim reported after the reporting window has closed is declined as a late report.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
