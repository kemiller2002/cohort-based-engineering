# CLM-07: Intake triage

Requirements: CLM-07-R1, CLM-07-R2, CLM-07-R3. Owner: Claims Operations.

## CLM-07-R1

Each claim is routed on intake. A claim whose reported amount is within the fast-track limit in force is settled
from the documents the claimant sends, without an adjuster visit. Every other claim is assigned an adjuster.

## CLM-07-R2

Each new claim opens with the initial reserve for its peril, under the reserving decision in force. The intake
summary shows the total reserved across the day's new claims.

## CLM-07-R3

The amount payable on a claim is the reported amount less the policy deductible, never below zero, held to the cent.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
