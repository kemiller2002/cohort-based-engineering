# PAY-08: Gross pay

Requirements: PAY-08-R1, PAY-08-R2. Owners: Payroll; People Operations.

## PAY-08-R1

For each hourly employee and week, gross pay is regular pay plus overtime pay plus statutory holiday pay, under the
decisions in force.

## PAY-08-R2

A salaried employee is paid an equal share of the annual salary each pay period, rounded to the cent.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
