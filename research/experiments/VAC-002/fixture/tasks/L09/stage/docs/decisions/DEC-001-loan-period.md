# DEC-001: Loan period

Status: accepted (2026-01-12, owner: Head of Lending). Revision 2 (2026-03-01, Head of
Lending): due dates fall on open days.

A loan lasts 21 days. The due date is the day the copy is borrowed plus 21 days. When
the library is closed on that day (a Sunday or a public holiday, as the OpenDays
calendar reports), the due date is the next day the library is open.
