# FRT-17: Delivery promise and shipment invoice

Requirements: FRT-17-R1, FRT-17-R2. Owners: Customer Experience; Logistics Pricing.

## FRT-17-R1

Each shipment confirmation states the promised delivery date: the ship date plus the transit days of the shipment's
service level under the service-level decision in force, in calendar days.

## FRT-17-R2

Each shipment invoice is the base charge plus the fuel surcharge in force.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
