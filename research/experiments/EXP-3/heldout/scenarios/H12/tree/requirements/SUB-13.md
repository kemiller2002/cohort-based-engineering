# SUB-13: Cancellations and seats

Requirements: SUB-13-R1, SUB-13-R2. Owner: Customer Success.

## SUB-13-R1

Cancelling a subscription refunds what the refund decision in force allows, and nothing else.

## SUB-13-R2

Adding seats that would take a subscription past its plan's seat limit under the seat decision in force is refused.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
