# DEC-004: Renewals

Status: accepted (2026-01-12, owner: Head of Lending). Revision 2 (2026-03-01, Head of
Lending): renewed due dates fall on open days.

A loan may be renewed at most 2 times. Each renewal moves the due date 21 days
later than the current due date; when the library is closed on that day, the due date
is the next day the library is open (as in DEC-001). A loan cannot be renewed:

1. when it is overdue (today is after its due date);
2. when it has already been renewed 2 times;
3. when any member holds the copy's book.

When several of these apply, the first one in this list is the reason given.
