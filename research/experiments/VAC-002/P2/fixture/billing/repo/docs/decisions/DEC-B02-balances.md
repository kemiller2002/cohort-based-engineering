# DEC-B02: Customer balances

Status: accepted (2026-01-05, owner: Finance)

A customer's balance is what they owe: the sum of what is outstanding on their invoices. A balance never goes below
zero. Money a customer is owed back (for example from a credit larger than their balance) is held as a refundable
credit; only Finance releases a refundable credit, either as a refund or by applying it to a named invoice.
