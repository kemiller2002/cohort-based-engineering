# PRC-03: Requisition routing

Requirements: PRC-03-R1, PRC-03-R2. Owner: Procurement Office.

## PRC-03-R1

Each requisition is routed to the lowest approver whose limit, under the approval-limit decision in force, covers its
total. A requisition above the director's limit goes to the CFO.

## PRC-03-R2

Each requisition shows how many competitive bids it needs under the bidding decision in force and any granted
approval.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
