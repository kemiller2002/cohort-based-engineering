# FRT-21: Transit cover and oversize handling

Requirements: FRT-21-R1, FRT-21-R2, FRT-21-R3. Owner: Risk and Pricing.

## FRT-21-R1

A shipment may carry transit cover. The premium for each parcel follows the transit cover decision in force.

## FRT-21-R2

The shipment summary shows our liability for each parcel without transit cover, and the number of parcels.

## FRT-21-R3

An oversize parcel pays the oversize handling fee for its delivery zone under the oversize decision in force.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
