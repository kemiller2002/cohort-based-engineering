# PAY-14: Payslip deductions and accruals

Requirements: PAY-14-R1, PAY-14-R2, PAY-14-R3. Owner: Payroll.

## PAY-14-R1

Each payslip shows the union dues deducted for the period under the dues decision in force. An employee who is not a
union member pays no dues.

## PAY-14-R2

Each payslip shows the employer pension contribution for the period under the pension decision in force.

## PAY-14-R3

Each payslip shows the vacation hours accrued in the period. An employee on unpaid leave accrues no vacation.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
