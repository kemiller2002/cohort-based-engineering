# L13: Loan periods in the member view

Requirements: LEND-L13-R1, LEND-L13-R2. Owner: Head of Lending.

## LEND-L13-R1

`member <member>` also shows the loan period that applies to the member, after their standing:
`m1 Ada Lovelace (standard), active, loan period 21 days` or
`m1 Ada Lovelace (standard), suspended: damaged books, loan period 21 days`.

## LEND-L13-R2

The loan period is defined once, per member type, in the lending policy, and borrowing uses that same definition, so
that the member view and the due date of a new loan can never disagree.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; the standing requirements in `README.md` hold; existing behaviour
is unchanged except as these requirements say; `dotnet test Lending.slnx` passes.
