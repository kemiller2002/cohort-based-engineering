# CLM-19: Motor total loss and rental allowance

Requirements: CLM-19-R1, CLM-19-R2, CLM-19-R3. Owner: Motor Claims.

## CLM-19-R1

A vehicle is a total loss when its repair estimate reaches the total-loss ratio in force of its market value.

## CLM-19-R2

A total loss is settled at the market value less the expected salvage value under the salvage decision in force.
Any other vehicle claim is settled at the repair estimate.

## CLM-19-R3

While a vehicle is being repaired the claimant is reimbursed for a rental car under the rental decision in force.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
