# FRT-05: Rate quotes

Requirements: FRT-05-R1, FRT-05-R2. Owner: Logistics Pricing.

## FRT-05-R1

A quote prices a shipment: the chargeable weight of each parcel times the rate per kilogram in force, summed, and at
least the minimum charge per shipment.

## FRT-05-R2

The quote adds the surcharge for the shipment's delivery zone under the zone decision in force.

Acceptance criteria: each statement above holds; existing behaviour is unchanged except as these requirements say.
